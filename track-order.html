Order tracking — questions

How do I track my order?

We send your tracking number via WhatsApp once your order is dispatched. Enter that number on the TCS, Leopards or M&P tracking page above.

Which courier handles my order?

We choose between TCS, Leopards and M&P based on your city and daily availability. The tracking number we send tells you which courier to check.

When does dispatch happen?

We dispatch on the same business day as payment confirmation, usually before end of day. Orders confirmed after 3 PM may dispatch the following morning.

My parcel shows delivered but I didn't receive it.

Contact us on WhatsApp with your order number and tracking number. We will open an inquiry with the courier and resolve it as quickly as possible.

Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?

Contact us on WhatsApp immediately. If your parcel has not yet been booked with the courier, we can update your address. Once dispatched, address changes depend on the courier and are not always possible.
