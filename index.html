What is included in a Chef Knife set?

Each Chef Knife set includes an 8-inch chef knife and a matching 3.5-inch paring knife, presented in a black gift box. The chef knife handles slicing, dicing and mincing; the paring knife is for precision work.

Where are Chef Knife chef knives made?

Every Chef Knife is forged, ground and finished by hand at the Pakistan Knife Factory (PKF) workshop on Fatimah Jinnah Road, Sialkot — the heart of Pakistan's blade-making heritage.

Do you deliver chef knife sets across Pakistan?

Yes. We offer free delivery to every city in Pakistan — Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Peshawar, Quetta, Sialkot and beyond. Orders arrive within 3 to 5 business days.

How do I pay for a chef knife set online?

Payment is in advance via EasyPaisa or JazzCash. Scan our QR code at checkout, send the full amount, upload the screenshot — and we confirm and ship within 24 hours.

What steel are Chef Knife chef knives made of?

High-carbon stainless steel, single-piece blade construction, hand-ground and polished. They take a fine edge and stay sharp through everyday kitchen use.
